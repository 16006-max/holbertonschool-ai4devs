# Product Idea

## Vision
CarpoolConnect is a corporate commute management platform that helps companies organize employee carpooling at scale. By matching colleagues who live and work near each other, it reduces commuting costs, parking pressure, and carbon emissions while making the daily commute more social and reliable.

## Users
- **Employees (Drivers)**: want to share fuel and parking costs and use their empty seats, with a predictable schedule and trusted colleagues as passengers.
- **Employees (Passengers)**: want an affordable, reliable, and stress-free alternative to driving alone or using crowded public transport.
- **Fleet / Facilities Managers**: want to reduce parking demand, manage shuttle and carpool resources, and see how employees commute.
- **HR and Sustainability Teams**: want to support employee wellbeing, meet ESG and carbon-reduction goals, and report on measurable impact.
- **Company Administrators**: want secure onboarding, company-only access, and control over policies and incentives.

## Key Features
- **Company-verified accounts**: sign-up with a corporate email or SSO, so rides are only shared among verified colleagues.
- **Smart ride matching**: matches drivers and passengers by home area, work site, shift schedule, and detour tolerance.
- **Recurring commute scheduling**: set up daily or weekly rides once, with easy handling of days off, remote work, and schedule changes.
- **Real-time tracking and notifications**: live pickup status, delay alerts, and ride confirmations.
- **Cost sharing and payments**: automatic fuel-cost splitting, or company-sponsored subsidies and mobility allowances.
- **Safety and trust**: ratings and reviews, profile verification, emergency contact sharing, and an in-app report function.
- **Parking and incentives management**: priority parking spots, rewards, and points for carpoolers.
- **Eco-impact tracking**: per-user and company-wide dashboards for CO2 saved, kilometers shared, and money saved.
- **Admin dashboard and analytics**: commute heatmaps, participation rates, and exportable ESG reports.
- **Guaranteed ride home**: a backup option (taxi or shuttle voucher) if a carpool partner is unavailable.

## Differentiators
- **Built for companies, not the general public**: closed, trusted network of colleagues instead of strangers.
- **Employer integration**: connects with HR systems, shift schedules, parking systems, and corporate benefits.
- **Measurable business value**: turns commuting data into ESG reporting, cost savings, and facility planning insights.
- **Reliability focus**: recurring rides and a guaranteed ride home remove the main reason employees avoid carpooling.
