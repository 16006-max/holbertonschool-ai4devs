# MVP Concept - TaskMaster AI (Smart Sprint & Task Planner)

## Problem Statement
Small software teams and early-stage startups waste hours manually breaking down product requirements into actionable tasks, estimating effort, and tracking sprint bottlenecks across fragmented tools.

## Target Users
- Early-stage startup founders and solo builders
- Engineering team leads and project managers
- Freelance developers managing client deliverables

## Core Features
- **AI Task Breakdown:** Paste a raw feature idea or product requirement and automatically generate structured tasks with priority levels and time estimates.
- **Interactive Kanban Board:** Drag-and-drop task management organized into *To Do*, *In Progress*, and *Done* columns.
- **Smart Sprint Filter & Search:** Filter tasks by priority, status, or keyword in real time.
- **Progress & Velocity Summary:** Lightweight dashboard metrics showing completed tasks, remaining story points, and overdue items.
- **JSON/CSV Export:** One-click export of the current sprint board for sharing with stakeholders or importing into external tools.

## Constraints
- Single-workspace scope (no multi-organization enterprise permissions in v1)
- No third-party integrations (e.g., Jira, Slack, or GitHub sync excluded from MVP)
- Maximum limit of 200 active tasks per project workspace
- Web-only responsive interface (no native iOS/Android mobile apps)
